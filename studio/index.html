Recommended: photos below 3 MB each; song below 12 MB. PNG/WebP with transparent background works best for a custom cake or candle.

Important for the final Netlify link

Studio uploads are intentionally browser-local preview overrides; a static Netlify site cannot write back into your GitHub project files. For the final link that your nephew/family opens on other phones, copy your real files into assets/photos/ or assets/uploads/, place your song in assets/audio/, then update the paths in config/birthday-config.js and push to GitHub. The included CUSTOMIZE.md shows the exact filenames and properties.
